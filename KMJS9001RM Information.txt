Device-specific

3. Important distinction for Samsung
The values above are general UFS 3.1 operating ranges, not verified Samsung KMJS9001RM-specific electrical limits.

Some embedded UFS devices also support a lower VCC operating range of 1.7–1.95 V. Therefore, confirm the Samsung device's supported power configuration before applying power.
